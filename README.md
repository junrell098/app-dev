# My Favorite Movies & Series

Here is a collection of my all-time favorite films and series across fantasy, sci-fi, and drama:

## 1. The Lord of the Rings
* **Genre:** Epic High Fantasy
* **Why I Love It:** The world-building of *Middle-earth*, unforgettable characters like Aragorn and Frodo, and an unmatched cinematic score.

## 2. Star Wars
* **Genre:** Space Opera / Sci-Fi
* **Why I Love It:** Iconic lightsaber duels, deep lore surrounding the Jedi and the Sith, and timeless storytelling across generations.

## 3. Harry Potter
* **Genre:** Fantasy / Adventure
* **Why I Love It:** Growing up alongside the characters at *Hogwarts*, exploring the Wizarding World, and themes of loyalty and courage.

## 4. The Golden Scenery of Tomorrow
* **Genre:** Filipino Romance / Drama
* **Why I Love It:** Adapted from the Wattpad novel by **4reuminct**, it captures a heartfelt story of friendship, college life, and chasing dreams with *Via* and *Larkin*.
